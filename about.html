OUR STORY

We're So Glad You're Here

Hi, it's Anna and Taylor, co-founders of Muddy Body!

As besties turned business owners, we created Muddy Body with the mission of empowering people to feel confident in their own skin each and every day. We believe that natural skincare should be simple, effective, and accessible to everyone, and every product is handmade with that goal in mind.

It all started when we met at Florida International University and hit it off immediately, thanks to our deep-rooted passion and appreciation for all things natural beauty. We bonded over our love for skincare and makeup and often found ourselves talking about the latest products we were trying out. Throughout our time in college, we bonded over this shared passion and became inseparable.

We knew we wanted to pursue our passion for skincare and make a difference in the industry, so we decided to become partners and start Muddy Body from our garage in Miami, FL. We started with a small budget and a big dream, initially dedicating all our resources to our line of natural, clay-based masks. Our masks quickly swept the nation, going viral on social media and gaining a strong following online. This motivated us to continue expanding our product line using the same natural, holistic approach we used for our clay masks--and Muddy Body soon became a skincare favorite internationally!

Now, a few years later, Muddy Body continues to be a living testament to the power of friendship. Today we have an amazing team who share our passion for skincare and are constantly coming up with innovative ways to help people get healthy skin in a completely natural way. We are so grateful for you and all of our customers who have supported us from the beginning, and we can't wait to continue providing you with the best natural skincare products available!

Our Promise

We want you to fall in love with Muddy Body, which is why we are committed to setting the standard for real, clean beauty by using only the purest ingredients that nature has to offer. That is why our products are cruelty-free, ethically sourced, vegan derived, and always made in the United States. We are dedicated to giving our customers a safe space to shop and explore all things natural skincare without making any compromises so that they can feel confident about what they are putting on their skin

Muddy Body is our way of bringing the healing power of nature to your doorsteps, and we are so excited to share this journey with you!
